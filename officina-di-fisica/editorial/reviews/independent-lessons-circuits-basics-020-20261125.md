## Individual reading and derivations

- **C4-L05 — Quanto vale una corrente (7 blocks).** Signed transported charge defines current; electron charge and drift have opposite signs to conventional positive current. A fixes C/s; V/I has ohm units. Derive R=rho*length/area from J=E/rho, I=JA and V=E*length for a homogeneous uniform wire at fixed temperature. V(I) slope is R, I(V) slope is 1/R. The lesson explicitly defines DeltaV in the resistor law as the passive voltage drop, so it does not silently reuse an opposite oriented potential difference. Manual reviewer probes: 6C/3s=2A, -6C/3s=-2A, 6V/3ohm=2A; doubled length doubles R and doubled radius quarters R. Self-heating can invalidate constant rho, as the text warns. Ohm SVG has a positive straight line through the origin and a correctly oriented slope triangle; there is no numerical axis calibration from which pixel ratios should be read as ohms.
- **C4-L06 — Seguire i nodi di un circuito (7 blocks).** Series means unbranched common node; parallel means the same two nodes. Adding drops at common current gives R1+R2; adding currents at common voltage gives R1R2/(R1+R2), smaller than each positive branch resistance. Passive VI agrees with I²R and V²/R. E=P*duration is explicitly restricted to constant power. Manual reviewer values 2ohm/3ohm give series5ohm, parallel6/5ohm; 6V*2A=12W and 12W*3s=36J. Boundary probe P(t)=2t over 0..3s gives9J rather than18J. A fixed-voltage source with another series resistor makes an individual member's power nonmonotonic as its resistance changes; this does not contradict the text's common-current comparison within one circuit. The diagram has the stated two topologies and no unintended shorting path.
- **C4-L07 — Immagazzinare carica ed energia (8 blocks).** Q is the magnitude on one plate, not the sum of both magnitudes. Uniform-field parallel-plate geometry gives C=epsilon*A/d. Linear charging work integrates q/C from zero to Q, yielding Q²/(2C)=QV/2=CV²/2 and joule units. The printed epsilon0 is explicitly approximate. Manual reviewer probe C2F,V3V gives Q6C,U9J. A dielectric factor2 gives U9/2J at fixedQ, U18J at fixedV. In the latter ideal quasistatic insertion, battery work18J exceeds field increase9J; the difference belongs in the mechanical balance, consistent with the lesson's explicit source/work qualification. The figure's five field arrows point from +Q to -Q. Facing area and separation are qualitative schematic labels, without a physical pixel scale.
- **C4-L08 — Mettere insieme i condensatori (7 blocks).** Parallel charges add at common voltage. Series equal charge magnitudes follow from the explicitly initially neutral, isolated floating middle node, not from geometry alone. Reciprocal addition gives the smaller equivalent capacitance; the smaller component gets the greater voltage. Manual reviewer 2F/3F yields parallel5F and series6/5F. Joining disconnected charged conductors conserves algebraic group charge: Vf=(C1V1+C2V2)/(C1+C2). Energy loss after settling is C1C2(V1-V2)²/[2(C1+C2)], including zero for equal initial voltage. Equal capacitors with opposite voltage cancel, rather than add charge magnitudes.
