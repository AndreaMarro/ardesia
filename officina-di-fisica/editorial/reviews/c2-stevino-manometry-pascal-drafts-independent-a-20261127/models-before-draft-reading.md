# Independent reconstruction before opening the four new drafts or Root worked manual

Exposure: A previously reviewed the original 047 pressure models and historical UI findings. This is reuse with declared exposure, not a claim of blind first discovery. For this new review only binding source IDs/ref were opened initially, then immutable original controls/references and actual current UI model source were read. The new candidate records, keys, explanations and Root manual have not yet been opened. No browser is executed in this review.

SIM-C2-L002-02: the control is upward height z above the bottom of a tank of depth H=3 m, not downward depth. At the free surface relative pressure is zero. A horizontal column equilibrium gives p_rel=ρg(H−z). The references imply ρg=10 kPa/m, consistent with water 1000 kg/m³ and assigned g=10 m/s². Therefore p_rel[kPa]=10(3−z[m]) for 0≤z≤3. At bottom 30 kPa, at surface zero; increasing z decreases pressure. Inverse z=3−p_rel/10, with 0≤p_rel≤30. Absolute pressure would additionally require surface pressure, which is not the delivered measure.

SIM-C2-L002-03: Torricelli barometer equilibrium is p_air−p_top=ρ_Hg g h with h the vertical difference from reservoir free surface to the column top. The model assumes negligible pressure above the column, static mercury, constant density and assigned g. The references yield 68/50=102/75=34/25 kPa/cm. Hence h[cm]=(25/34)p_air[kPa], inverse p_air=(34/25)h. This is consistent with ρ_Hg=13600 kg/m³, g=10 m/s² and 1 cm=1/100 m: each centimetre contributes1360 Pa=1.36 kPa. The assigned coefficient is not silently the standard Earth-gravity value. Domain p_air in [68,544/5] kPa gives h in[50,80] cm. It is a barometer, not the draining-tank efflux law also called Torricelli.

SIM-C2-L002-04: at equal elevation in the same static manometric liquid, pressures agree. Writing pressure plus ρgy constant gives p_L−p_R=ρg(y_R−y_L). Thus if Δp=p_L−p_R and Δh=y_R−y_L, Δh[cm]=10Δp[kPa] for assigned ρ=1000 kg/m³,g=10 m/s². Positive left excess depresses the left level and raises the right. For equal cross sections each level shifts half the signed difference: right +5Δp cm, left −5Δp cm, relative to equal-pressure levels. A signed difference is not the nonnegative magnitude and not a single-arm displacement. Original domain Δp∈[−2,2] kPa corresponds Δh∈[−20,20] cm; zero is included.

SIM-C2-L003-01: Pascal transmits an added pressure uniformly through confined static fluid, independently of the pre-existing hydrostatic pressure. Added force on a face is ΔF=Δp A. Original references imply large area A₂=2 N/kPa=2/1000 m²=20 cm². With A₂=4A₁, small area A₁=5 cm² and ΔF₁=(1/2)Δp N when the numerical pressure is in kPa; ΔF₂=2Δp N, ratio4 for positive pressure. At zero both added forces are zero, so a ratio0/0 must not be asserted there. Scope Δp∈[0,10] kPa, ΔF₂∈[0,20] N. Work/energy or displacement gains do not follow from Pascal alone; under ideal volume conservation the larger face moves less. Arrow lengths need an explicit graphical convention, and cannot establish force magnitude under axial projection.

All coefficients are exact rational numbers in this reconstruction. Every future task will be independently substituted into these laws and checked dimensionally, including signs and prefixes. No draft is approved by this file.
