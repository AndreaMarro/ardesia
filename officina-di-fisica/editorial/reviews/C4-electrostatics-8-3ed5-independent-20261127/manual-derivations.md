## SIM-C4-L01-01

Two initially neutral objects exchange an integer N electrons, with no other charge transfer. Removing negative electrons makes A positive: q_A=+Ne, q_B=-Ne, total zero. The answer requested is q_A/e, a pure coefficient, not coulombs. The actual first task N=6 gives +6; transfer N=4 gives +4. At N=0 both are neutral. Negative N, fractional electrons and loss of the closed-system assumption are outside this model. An elementary-charge approximation in the explanation is not needed for either numeric answer.

## SIM-C4-L03-01

An equilateral triangle of side 1 cm has exactly three pairs. With the first two charges +1 nC and the third q_3 nC, the common unit factor is k(10^-9 C)^2/(10^-2 m)=9×10^-7 J=.9 µJ. Pair sum is 1+q_3+q_3=1+2q_3. The actual first task q_3=2 gives .9×5=4.5 µJ; transfer q_3=.5 gives .9×2=1.8 µJ. q_3=-.5 gives zero total pair energy, even though individual pair energies are nonzero. The energy zero is at infinite separation; self energies are excluded and each pair is counted once. Negative total energy is allowed.
