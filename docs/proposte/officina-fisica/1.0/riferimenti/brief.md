## Brief comune

Design reference for one actual Italian educational product, senior editorial/product design. A wide 3:2 design sheet with two parallel views of the SAME first screen: on the left large edge-to-edge desktop interface, on the right 390px mobile adaptation separated by a hairline. No device mockups, drop-shadowed presentation frames, hero photos, numbers for decoration, extra words, dashboards, badges, mascots, gradients, invented copy or unrelated pages. Very readable. Actual product interface, components buildable with accessible HTML and CSS. The irregular signed velocity graph is pedagogical: from t=0 to 4 s velocity +3 m/s, then from 4 to 9 s velocity -2 m/s, clear zero axis, two regions and time labels 0,4,9. Prioritize hierarchy over fitting labels. No solved answer in the initial screen.
