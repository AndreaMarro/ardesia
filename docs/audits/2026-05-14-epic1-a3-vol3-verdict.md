# EPIC 1 A3 — Volume 3 book-faithfulness verdict

**Method note.** For each of the 29 `v3-*` experiments I read its `alignment-records.json` record (repo claim, Tea claim, repo `volumeRef` citation, evidence flags), then searched the verbatim physical-volume extract `CONTENUTI/volumi-pdf/vol3-v0.8.1.txt` myself with grep + offset reads. Ground truth is the volume text only — neither the repo `lesson-paths` nor Tea's `narrative_map` was trusted. Key structural finding up front: **Volume 3 V0.8.1 contains real content only for Chapters 5–8 (printed pages ~47–83). Chapters 9–12 are empty shells** (blank pages with only a placeholder title like "COSA SONO I FOTOTRANSISTOR?"). Each chapter holds a *small* number of numbered `ESPERIMENTO`/`ESERCIZIO` blocks — Ch5: ~2, Ch6: 5 (ESP 1–5), Ch7: ESERCIZIO 7.3 + "Cambia un numero" + 7.8 mini-progetto, Ch8: one ESERCIZIO 8.x ("Trova A0") + one analogRead program. The repo defines 29 `v3-*` experiments and Tea's map claims 29 — **both inventories are inflated/misaligned versus the ~14 experiment blocks actually printed in the volume.** As a consequence Tea's `bookText` is fabricated for all 29 records (the A2 headline "0/29 v3 teaBookText found in volume" is confirmed by my own search — none of Tea's `bookText` strings appear verbatim, and the long "ESERCIZIO 7.1 / 8.1" passages Tea quotes do not exist in this edition at all).

The repo's `lesson-paths` titles are *thematically* much closer to the volume than Tea's titles for Ch5–Ch8 core material, but the repo also invents experiments (PWM/Fade/Knight Rider/debounce, the three Extra capstones, the standalone "Serial message" Ch8 closer) that have **no counterpart in the printed volume**. Pages: the volume prints chapter-start pages 47 (Ch5), 53 (Ch6), 63 (Ch7), 75 (Ch8); per-experiment page numbers below are my best cross-check against the footer page markers in the text — Tea's and the repo's fine-grained page numbers (57/58/59, 76–80, 81–87, 88–92) are mostly **unverifiable or impossible** (e.g. pages 84+ are blank).

## Divergences requiring Andrea decision

These are cases where repo and Tea disagree AND the volume text does not cleanly settle which fine-grained mapping is correct (the *chapter-level* truth is settled — the volume clearly does not match Tea — but the *experiment-level* identity/page is ambiguous).

1. **v3-cap5-esp2** — repo "Modifica tempi del Blink" vs Tea "Interruttori in serie" (p.49). The volume DOES contain a "cambia la velocità del lampeggio / modifica il delay (200ms veloce, 2000ms lento)" exercise, which matches the repo — but the volume prints it as an *"Esperimento aggiuntivo"* attached to Chapter 6 (line 1067–1075), not as a standalone Ch5 experiment on page 49. So the title is repo-faithful but its page/chapter placement is ambiguous. Tea is simply wrong (no "interruttori in serie" exists).

2. **v3-cap6 experiment count and ids** — the volume Chapter 6 has exactly **five** numbered experiments (ESP 1–5). The repo declares for Ch6: esp1–esp7 + `morse` + `semaforo` (9 ids); Tea declares 9 as well. The repo's named ids `v3-cap6-morse` and `v3-cap6-semaforo` clearly map to volume ESP 3 and ESP 5 — but then `v3-cap6-esp3` ("2 LED alternati") and `v3-cap6-esp5` ("Sirena con Buzzer") become duplicates/inventions. Which id set should survive consolidation needs Andrea's decision; the volume cannot tell us which repo id is the "canonical" Morse/semaforo entry.

3. **v3-cap7-esp2 vs v3-cap7-esp6 vs the Ch7 "mini-progetto"** — the volume Chapter 7 has one mini-progetto ("Due LED, un pulsante, Arduino sceglie", line 1498) and one "Cambia un numero" exercise (line 1425). The repo spreads Ch7 across 8 ids + `v3-cap7-mini`; Tea across 8 ids + `v3-cap7-mini`. Neither repo nor Tea titles for esp2 ("Pulsante spegne LED" / "Potenziometro e controllo analogico") cleanly equal the volume's mini-progetto, and it is ambiguous whether the volume's mini-progetto should be `v3-cap7-esp2` or `v3-cap7-mini`. Andrea must decide the collapse.

4. **v3-cap8 experiment split** — the volume Chapter 8 contains essentially **one** worked analog-read program (declare `int valore`, `Serial.begin` in setup, `analogRead(A0)` + `Serial.println(valore)` + `delay(200)` in loop) plus the "Trova A0 / prepara la breadboard" setup steps. The repo splits this into 5 ids (`esp1`–`esp5`) + `serial`; Tea into 6 ids with completely unrelated Ohm's-law titles. The volume cannot support 5–6 distinct Ch8 experiments — which (if any) repo ids to keep needs Andrea's decision. (`v3-cap8-esp4`, `esp5`, `serial` and Tea's `esp1`-`esp5` titles have no volume basis at all and should likely be dropped, not merely re-titled — flagging because dropping experiments is a product decision.)

5. **The 11 "unverified — no volume counterpart" experiments** (`v3-cap6-esp6/esp7`, `v3-cap7-esp4/esp5/esp7/esp8`, `v3-cap7-mini` as a Ch7 item, `v3-cap8-esp4/esp5/serial`, all 3 `v3-extra-*`) — these are software experiments with no printed equivalent in Volume 3 V0.8.1. This is the biggest divergence: it is not a title disagreement, it is the **software shipping ~15 experiments the current volume edition does not cover.** Andrea must decide whether these are (a) legitimate additions awaiting a future volume edition, or (b) drift to be removed for the volume↔kit↔software moat. I cannot settle this from the volume — the volume is simply silent.
