## How to read the "verbatim printed book title" column

The volumes are inconsistent: some experiments have a real printed title on the same line/next line as the `ESPERIMENTO N` / `ESERCIZIO` marker, others have **only** `ESPERIMENTO N` followed by a descriptive sentence. Where there is a real printed title it is quoted verbatim. Where there is only a marker + sentence, that is stated explicitly and the sentence is quoted as the basis for the proposed clean title.

OCR note: the extracted text mangles digits/accents (e.g. `E` for `LED`, `1` for `13`, ` ai un` for `Fai un SOS`). Quotes below are de-OCR'd to the obvious intended Italian; the line citation lets you confirm.

## Notes — ambiguous cases & contract divergences

### Cases where the printed book has NO clear title (judgment call needed)

These experiments are printed as a bare `ESPERIMENTO N` marker followed only by a descriptive sentence — there is **no printed short title** to copy verbatim. The proposed clean title is a faithful condensation of the book's own sentence, not an invention, but Andrea should ratify the wording:

- **`v1-cap9-esp4`, `v1-cap9-esp5`, `v1-cap9-esp8`** (Vol1 Ch9) — all bare markers + sentence.
- **`v1-cap10-esp2`, `v1-cap10-esp3`, `v1-cap10-esp5`** (Vol1 Ch10) — all bare markers + sentence.
- **`v1-cap11-esp1`, `v1-cap11-esp2`** (Vol1 Ch11) — bare markers; esp1 has only a materials list, no descriptive sentence even, so the proposed title is derived from the chapter topic + the "Collega ora il cicalino alla batteria" body line.
- **`v1-cap12-esp1..4`** (Vol1 Ch12) — all bare markers + sentence.
- **`v1-cap13-esp2`** (Vol1 Ch13) — bare marker + sentence.
- **`v3-cap5-esp1`, `v3-cap5-esp2`** (Vol3 Ch5) — Ch5 has **zero** `ESPERIMENTO` markers; it is a single continuous walk-through of the Blink sketch. The repo's 2-way split (esp1 "Blink", esp2 "modifica tempi") is a software-side decomposition. The proposed titles are derived from the chapter's own framing ("il programma Blink") and the Ch6 "Esperimenti aggiuntivi — Cambia la velocità del lampeggio" block.
- **`v3-cap8-esp1`, `v3-cap8-esp2`** (Vol3 Ch8) — Ch8 contains **one continuous worked analog-read program**, not discrete experiments. Only `v3-cap8-esp3` has a real printed marker (`ESERCIZIO 8.x Trova A0`). The esp1/esp2 split is editorial; the proposed titles reflect the printed section headings ("Primo programma..." for esp1, the `void loop` analogRead/println section for esp2) but the split itself needs Andrea's ratification (recon §G open question 1).

1. **`v3-cap6-esp1`** — lesson-path `title` is `"Circuito AND/OR con pulsanti"`. **This experiment does not exist in Vol3.** Ch6 ESPERIMENTO 1 (vol3 line 1002) is "Colleghiamo la resistenza" — the first external LED + resistance on the breadboard. The contract already corrects this; the lesson-path title is fully fabricated.

3. **`v3-cap6-morse` & `v3-cap6-semaforo`** — the contract `bookFaithfulTitle` for these two `keep` entries contains an editorial meta-note in parentheses: `"... (duplicate of v3-cap6-esp3 in repo's named-id scheme)"` / `"... (duplicate of v3-cap6-esp5 in named-id scheme)"`. **Applying the contract value verbatim would write "duplicate of v3-cap6-esp3 in repo's named-id scheme" into a user-facing title.** The proposed clean titles strip the meta-note: "Esperimento 3: fai un SOS in codice Morse" / "Esperimento 5: il semaforo". The contract author should fix `a3-corrections.json` for these two entries (recon §G open question 4).
4. **`v3-cap5-esp2` placement** — the contract itself flags (`confidence: "medium"`) that the "cambia velocità del lampeggio" exercise is printed as an *Esperimento aggiuntivo attached to Ch6* (vol3 lines 1051–1055), **not** as a standalone Ch5 p.49 experiment.
